# Unison enclosure design pathway

Status: concept requirements only

The enclosure is a brand and serviceability boundary, not decorative packaging.
It must support modular front-service blades, keyed replacement, independent
power isolation, filtered directed airflow, accessible status through native
conversation and tactile indicators, and safe professional installation.

No dimensions, materials, circuit requirement, cooling capacity, acoustic
claim, ingress rating, or installation method is qualified yet. CAD sources
must use open, editable formats. Generated manufacturing outputs must retain
source revision, units, tolerances, material, finish, and license metadata.
