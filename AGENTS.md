# Agent guide

- Start from `system/requirements.md` and `interfaces/registry.yaml`.
- Do not select a component solely because it is available in 2026; record the
  requirement, interface, envelope, alternatives, lifecycle, and uncertainty.
- Every BOM item needs source, date, currency, quantity, lifecycle, estimate
  status, power/thermal contribution, and substitution notes.
- Never claim electrical, thermal, RF, mechanical, accessibility, fabrication,
  or safety qualification without revision-bound physical evidence.
- Do not add licensed vendor CAD or documentation without redistribution rights.
- No fabrication or redistribution license is granted until the owner selects
  the hardware license recorded in `LICENSE-STATUS.md`.
